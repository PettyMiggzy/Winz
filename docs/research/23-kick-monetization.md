# Research: kick-monetization

_Business-model sweep, research date 2026-08-15/16. Nearly every dollar figure in this space is self-reported by streamers who profit from inflating it — treat headline numbers as marketing, verified numbers are flagged as such._

## Summary

Kick's 95/5 sub split ($4.74 net per $4.99 sub, gifted subs included) is confirmed still live in mid-2026, but the real money engine is the Kick Partner Program (KPP), which pays per-stream, viewership-scaled "hourly" money (~$10/hr at ~50-75 CCV up to ~$100/hr at 1,000 CCV) — and it is gated at 75 average concurrent viewers, so 10-50-viewer channels earn essentially only subs and Kicks gifts ($50-500/month realistic). The $30K-100K/month mid-size stories (Sam Frank $80K, Cheesur $31K, Ac7ionMann $36K) are real but KPP/sub-driven at 500-3,500 CCV with extreme hour counts, and the whole pot is subsidized by Stake gambling profits; a November 2025 bot-cleanup repriced payouts (Adin Ross: $1M+/mo → $243K) showing program risk. For a small channel like WinslowBankz, the clips flywheel's near-term value is discovery to cross the 75-CCV KPP gate (the single biggest income step-change), plus modest off-Kick clip revenue (TikTok Creator Rewards $0.40-1.00 RPM but only >60s videos; Shorts $0.30-1.50 RPM), not direct platform revenue.

## Key facts

- Sub economics (verified, Aug 2026): $4.99/month base sub, 95/5 split = ~$4.74 to streamer; gifted subs pay the same 95%; higher tiers reported at $9.99/$24.99. CRITICAL CAVEAT: multistreaming to another platform cuts the split to ~50% for that broadcast.
- Kicks virtual currency (launched ~mid-2025): 100 Kicks ≈ $1.09 to the buyer; streamer receives ~95-96% of face value (roughly 1 cent per Kick). Payouts run monthly via Stripe/bank/PayPal with a reported ~$50 minimum (sources vary $50-75; historically $100) and 1-7 business day processing; KPP bonuses pay out after each stream.
- Tier ladder (2026, from secondary guides — Kick's own help pages blocked scraping): Affiliate/Creator = 75 followers + 5 streamed hours (+phone, 2FA, 2 VODs) and unlocks the full 95/5 subs immediately; Verified = 30 hours over 12 days in past 30 days, clean record; Partner (KPP) = 250 followers, 30 hrs/30 days, 75 average CCV, 25 active subs, 250 unique chatters, plus manual review.
- KPP pay is algorithmic per-stream, not a posted rate card: Sam Frank's on-stream breakdown (April 2025) = ~$10/hr at 50 viewers, ~$100/hr at 1,000 viewers; commonly cited 2025-26 band is $16-32/hr for qualifying channels; KCIP-era reports showed $3-15/hr swings. Kick says the program had paid $81,946,675 total as of July 2025.
- The 75-CCV gate means a 10-viewer or 50-viewer streamer gets NO hourly program money. Realistic platform-only earnings: 10 CCV ≈ $0-150/mo (5-15 subs + rare tips); 50 CCV ≈ $200-600/mo; 200 CCV (KPP-qualified) ≈ $3,000-5,000/mo (KPP ~$2-4K + subs ~$0.5-1.4K); 1,000 CCV ≈ $20,000-40,000/mo (KPP ~$10-15K + subs + tips). Cross-checks: Slightly Homeless $22K/mo at 780 CCV (Feb 2025); Cheesur $31K in March 2025 from 11 streams.
- The $30K-100K/month claims are real but concentrated and grind-heavy: Sam Frank $80K best month = KPP alone at ~3,500 CCV streaming ~15 hrs/day for ~30 days (self-reported on stream, April 2025); Ac7ionMann $36K in April 2025 from subs alone; top whales are platform-deal/gambling money — xQc's Kick deal was $70M/2yr (up to $100M) signed June 2023, and in March 2026 he said Stake pays $200K flat per sponsored gambling stream.
- November 2025 payout controversy: Kick deployed new view-authenticity detection, removed ~1,000 streamers from the program and deleted millions of bot accounts; Adin Ross's KPP fell from >$1M/mo (July 2025) to $243K and he publicly flirted with Twitch; Sneako reported $81 for a 2-hour stream; Asmongold reported no change. Eddie Craven: rates unchanged, only inauthentic views repriced.
- Kick 2026 trajectory is up: 500M+ hours watched in March 2026, record watch time in June 2026 (+22% MoM average viewership), ~659K average concurrent viewers (May 2026), 1.82M peak CCV (July 5, 2026), all-time 4.6M peak (WestCOL Stream Fighters 4, Oct 18, 2025), ~17% of tracked livestream hours — but Kick deliberately runs at a loss funded by Stake (Stake GGR $2.6B in 2022), and gambling regulation (e.g., Australia reforms effective Jan 1, 2027) is a structural risk.
- Clips-flywheel economics (2026): Whop Content Rewards clipping campaigns pay $0.20-$6 per 1,000 verified views (marketplace average ~$1; e.g., Roobet $1.50/1K with $250K budget); Vyro (MrBeast, launched Oct 2025) pays flat $3/1K with $1K/clip cap; Kick gambling-stream campaigns pay $10+/1K because clippers risk their accounts. This is what big Kick streamers PAY for — a small team clipping its own footage replicates it at zero campaign cost.
- Off-Kick monetization of own clips: TikTok Creator Rewards RPM $0.40-1.00 (up to $1.50 US) but ONLY videos >1 minute qualify — sub-60s clips earn $0; YouTube Shorts RPM $0.30-1.50; sponsor benchmark for live streams is $0.50-2.50 per concurrent viewer per hour (small 50-500 CCV channels: $100-800 per sponsored stream). Gambling-adjacent clips risk demonetization/bans on TikTok and YouTube.

## Gotchas / reality check

- Kick's official help-center pages (revenue split, KICKs guide, payout schedule) return 403 to scrapers — every threshold cited here (75 followers, 75 CCV, $50 minimum, tier prices) is from secondary 2026 guides and should be confirmed in the actual creator dashboard before planning around it.
- Nearly all headline earnings ($80K Sam Frank, $31K Cheesur, $36K Ac7ionMann, Adin's $1M) are SELF-REPORTED on stream and amplified by Kick-friendly clip accounts; Kick benefits from the hype. Only the aggregate $81.9M program total comes from Kick itself, and even that is unaudited marketing.
- Massive survivorship bias: the Dexerto/X success stories are the top ~0.1%; the median Kick channel has <5 viewers and earns ~$0. Sneako's $81-for-2-hours is the more representative post-repricing data point for non-elite channels.
- The KPP 'hourly rate' is not a rate card — it's an undisclosed per-stream algorithm Kick can (and did, Nov 2025) reprice without notice; ~1,000 streamers were removed outright. Never budget KPP money before it lands.
- The 75 average-CCV Partner gate is far harder than it sounds (roughly top 1-2% of channels); Affiliate at 75 followers is trivial but only unlocks subs. Any temptation to viewbot across the gate now leads to program removal under the new detection system.
- Multistreaming cuts the 95/5 split to ~50% — directly relevant if the clips strategy includes simulcasting to TikTok Live or YouTube.
- Kick's money is downstream of Stake casino profits and Kick admits running at a loss; regulatory shocks (Australia reforms Jan 2027, EU/UK pressure) could shrink the subsidy. Do not build a business plan assuming 2025-level KPP generosity persists.
- TikTok Creator Rewards pays $0 on videos under 60 seconds — the standard viral clip length — and both TikTok and YouTube demonetize/ban gambling-adjacent content, so any casino segments in WinslowBankz streams poison the off-platform funnel.
- Third-party earnings-ladder estimates (RankTracker's $260-320/mo at 10-30 CCV) assume implausibly high sub conversion (~50 subs); real small-channel conversion makes $0-150/mo more honest at 10 CCV.
- Reported payout minimum varies by source ($50 vs $75 vs legacy $100) and non-verified streamers may be limited to monthly payouts with possible 30% US tax withholding for non-treaty countries — cash flow at small scale is lumpy.

## Full details

# How streamers actually make money ON Kick in 2026 (small-scale focus)

## 1. Sub economics — 95/5 confirmed, with fine print

- **95/5 split is still live as of August 2026** (multiple guides re-verified this month; StreamScheme notes "rates verified August 2026"). Base sub **$4.99/mo → ~$4.74 to the streamer**. Reported Tier 2 ($9.99) and Tier 3 ($24.99) subs carry the same 95% share.
- **Gifted subs**: purchased 1-100 at a time, pay the streamer the **same 95%** as a direct sub — 100 gifted subs ≈ **$474**.
- **The multistream penalty is the biggest fine-print item**: broadcasting simultaneously to another platform cuts the effective share to **~50%** for that stream (Kick documentation, echoed in the Asmongold KPP coverage — his KPP is already halved because he multistreams). If WinslowBankz simulcasts to TikTok Live/YouTube, they forfeit the platform's headline advantage.
- **Kicks (virtual currency), launched ~mid-2025**: viewers buy Kicks bundles (**100 Kicks ≈ $1.09**) and send animated gifts; streamer redeems at **~95-96% of face value**, i.e. roughly a cent per Kick. Works below Partner level and is the fastest first-dollar path for tiny channels. (Kick's own help article "Guide to KICKs and Gifts" exists but blocks scraping — exact redemption table should be checked in the creator dashboard.)
- **Payouts**: monthly cycle; reported minimum **~$50** (sources range $50-75; older sources said $100 — treat as "check dashboard"); processing 1-7 business days via Stripe/bank/PayPal (one 2026 guide says Tipalti backend and warns of **30% US withholding absent a tax treaty**). **KPP bonuses pay after each stream** to the streamer's Stripe account (confirmed by Kick partner manager PIKE, Feb 2024). Verified streamers reportedly get faster/more frequent payouts than the monthly-only default.

## 2. The tier ladder: Affiliate → Verified → Partner

*(Kick's help.kick.com articles 403'd scraping; the following is triangulated from StreamScheme, Streams Charts, Boosthill, Gemlist and siege.gg 2026 guides — verify exact numbers in the dashboard.)*

| Tier | Requirements (2026) | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Affiliate / Creator Program** | **75 followers + 5 streamed hours**, verified phone, 2FA, 2 recent VODs (one source says the follower requirement was dropped in the **March 2025 overhaul** — conflicting reports) | Subs + gifted subs at 95/5, Kicks gifts, emotes |
| **Verified** | **30 hours streamed across 12 different days in past 30 days**, no TOS strikes, no recent username change; Affiliate first | Badge, better discovery placement, faster payout cadence, prerequisite for Partner |
| **Partner (KPP)** | **250 followers, 30 hrs/30 days, 75 average CCV, 25 active subs, 250 unique chatters** — then **manual review** (meeting minimums ≠ approval) | The hourly/per-stream Partner Income money |

**Program history**: KCIP began testing **Sept 2023 with 33 creators**; opened to applications **Feb 2024** (then-requirements: 100 CCV avg, 50 hrs across 15 days, 25 subs, 500 unique chatters). **March 2025**: Kick overhauled everything into "Path to Creator / Verification / Partner," dropping the viewership gate to **75 CCV**. Kick announced the program had paid **$81,946,675 cumulative as of July 2025** ("streamers out here making NBA money").

**How KPP actually pays**: not a flat wage — a **backend algorithm per stream** weighing CCV, unique chatters, chat velocity, and (per Streams Charts' Feb 19, 2026 write-up) an "Authority Score" for consistency/retention/guideline adherence. Observable data points:
- **Sam Frank's on-stream rate card (April 2025): ~$10/hour at 50 viewers, ~$100/hour at 1,000 viewers** — implying roughly $0.10/viewer-hour.
- Commonly cited qualifying band: **$16-32/hour**; KCIP-era reporting (Passionfruit) documented **$3-15/hour** session swings and "nobody really has a definite answer how it works."
- Early proof point: Luke "Majin" Rodriguez earned **~$3,000 for 155 hours across 39 streams in March 2024** (~$19/hr) and said he made more in 2 months on Kick than 3 years on Twitch.
- Streams Charts (Feb 2026) also mentions new **"Micro-Incentives"** — smaller performance bonuses for sub-75-CCV channels with high retention. **Single-source, unconfirmed** — worth watching, because it would be the first program money accessible to WinslowBankz's current size.

## 3. Realistic earnings ladder (platform revenue only)

The structural fact that dominates everything: **below 75 average CCV there is no hourly program money.** Platform income = subs + Kicks + tips only.

| Avg CCV | KPP? | Plausible platform income/month | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **10** | No | **$0-150** (5-15 subs ≈ $25-70 + occasional Kicks) | Sub conversion at tiny scale runs ~5-20% of CCV; RankTracker's "$260-320 at 10-30 CCV" assumes 50 subs — optimistic |
| **50** | No | **$200-600** (25-75 subs ≈ $120-350 + $50-200 gifts/tips) | Same sources; still zero hourly money |
| **75-100** (the gate) | Yes | **$1,000-2,500** — ~$10-16/hr × 100-150 hrs + subs | Sam Frank rate card low end; KCIP $16/hr floor claims |
| **200** | Yes | **$3,000-5,000** (KPP ~$2-4K + 100-300 subs = $475-1,400 + tips) | RankTracker mid-tier estimate $3,400-4,200 for 100-250 CCV is consistent |
| **1,000** | Yes | **$20,000-40,000** (KPP ~$100/hr × 100-150 hrs = $10-15K + 500-2,000 subs = $2.4-9.5K + tips/sponsors) | Cross-checked: Slightly Homeless **$22K in Feb 2025 at 780 CCV**; Cheesur **$31K in March 2025 from 11 streams**; RankTracker top-tier $30-40K at 1,000+ CCV |

Community-corroborated real months: xGEWNx **$11K KPP + ~$5K subs/TTS in Nov 2024** (typical months $3-5K); Trainwreck's own dashboard showed **$16K from ~3,500 subs** (matches 3,500 × $4.74). Counter-example: **Sneako earned $81 for a 2-hour stream** post-repricing (Nov 2025) — his response: "now I understand why so many streamers focus on gambling."

**Takeaway for WinslowBankz**: at 10-50 CCV, Kick platform revenue is beer money. The economic event that changes the channel is **crossing 75 average CCV and passing Partner review** — that single step is worth roughly **$1,000-2,000/month of algorithmic hourly pay** at their hour volume, more than every sub they could plausibly sell before it. The clips flywheel should be aimed squarely at that gate.

## 4. The $30K-100K/month claims — who and what composition

- **Sam Frank — $80,000 in her best month (reported on stream April 3-4, 2025)**: entirely **Partner Income program**, averaging ~3,500 viewers, streaming **~15 hours/day for almost 30 days** (~400+ hours). Self-reported with dashboard shown; heavily amplified by Kick-adjacent clip accounts. Note the labor input.
- **Cheesur — $31K (March 2025, 11 streams)**; **Ac7ionMann — $36K (April 2025, subs alone)**; **Slightly Homeless — $22K (Feb 2025, 780 CCV)**. All self-reported in a Dexerto roundup that reads as Kick PR-friendly.
- **Adin Ross**: KPP **>$1M in July 2025**, fell to **$243K in November 2025** after the authenticity repricing (he claims an 80% cut, called the program "outdated," said there's "no incentive to subscribe on Kick," started talks with Twitch — but remained one of Kick's most-watched through H1 2026). He also announced a **separate betting-site partnership in Sept 2025** — i.e., even his composition is platform program + gambling sponsor.
- **xQc**: **$70M/2yr deal (up to $100M with incentives), June 2023**; in **March 2026** he stated **Stake pays $200K flat per sponsored gambling stream** regardless of length, and referenced earlier eras where "a single gambling stream was around $15M" in sponsorship-era value. Amouranth signed a similar ~$100M-scale deal (2023).
- **Composition reality**: for whales it's **fixed platform/exclusivity deals + gambling sponsorships >> KPP >> subs** (Trainwreck: 3,500 subs = only $16K). For the mid-size $20-80K tier, it's **KPP + subs**, requiring 500-3,500 genuine CCV and extreme hours. Nobody at 10-200 CCV is making $30K from Kick itself; small channels touching that money are doing it via **gambling referral/kickback deals**, which carry reputational, legal (jurisdiction) and platform-dependency risk.

## 5. Kick 2025-2026 trajectory and sustainability

**Growth is real**: 500M+ hours watched in **March 2026** (best since Oct 2025); **June 2026 record watch time, average viewership +22% MoM**; average concurrents **~659K (May 2026)**; 30-day peak **1.82M CCV (July 5, 2026)**; all-time peak **4.6M CCV** at WestCOL's Stream Fighters 4 (**Oct 18, 2025**); ~11K live channels; ~**17% of tracked livestream hours** (aggregator-reported).

**Risks**:
- **Stake dependency**: Kick is owned by Stake co-founders Ed Craven/Bijan Tehrani; Craven has said Kick **deliberately operates at a loss** to build share. Stake's gross gaming revenue was **$2.6B (2022)** and reportedly multiples of that since — the KPP pot is effectively marketing spend for a crypto casino. Regulatory tightening (Stake banned in Australia for casino products; Australian online-gambling ad reforms announced **April 2, 2026, commencing Jan 1, 2027**) is the structural threat to that subsidy.
- **November 2025 repricing**: new detection for multi-tab/idle views, emote spam, chat-viewer mismatch; **~1,000 streamers removed from the program, millions of bot accounts deleted**. Craven insisted "creators with real audiences shouldn't see any meaningful drop." Lesson for a small channel: **any viewbotting to cross the 75-CCV gate = removal**, and program terms can be repriced unilaterally with no notice.
- Discovery on Kick still skews to gambling/IRL/drama meta; a small variety channel's organic Kick discovery is weak, which is exactly why an off-platform clips funnel matters.

## 6. Off-Kick income a grown channel unlocks (and the clips flywheel from both sides)

**The flywheel big streamers run**: they PAY clippers via campaign marketplaces. 2026 rates: **Whop Content Rewards $0.20-$6 per 1,000 verified views** (avg ~$1; e.g., Roobet $1.50/1K with a $250K budget); **Vyro (MrBeast/ViewStats, launched Oct 2025) flat $3/1K**, $1K cap/clip, hourly payouts; **Kick gambling-stream campaigns pay $10+/1K** because clippers risk their TikTok/YT accounts posting it. WinslowBankz's team doing this in-house on footage they own replicates the mechanism at zero campaign cost — and later, a $200-500 Whop campaign is a plausible paid-growth lever.

**Directly monetizing their own clips**:
- **TikTok Creator Rewards**: **$0.40-1.00 RPM** (up to ~$1.50 US audience) — but **only videos >60 seconds qualify**; classic sub-minute clips earn $0. Cut 61-90s edits for TikTok.
- **YouTube Shorts**: **$0.30-1.50 RPM** average (gaming/clips typically bottom of range). Long-form highlight compilations on a VOD channel carry much higher RPM (commonly cited $2-6 for gaming — not independently verified this session).
- **Warning**: gambling-adjacent content is demonetization/ban territory on both TikTok and YouTube — the same reason Kick clip campaigns pay a premium. If WinslowBankz's stream includes casino segments, keep them out of the off-platform funnel.
- **Sponsors**: live-stream benchmark **$0.50-2.50 per CCV per hour** (US); 50-500 CCV channels report **$100-800 per sponsored stream** ($50-300/hr). Below ~100 CCV expect product/affiliate-code deals, not cash.
- **Merch/community**: negligible until there's a recognizable identity; typically last to add.

**Recommended target mix at their scale**: treat clips primarily as a **viewer-acquisition engine toward the 75-CCV Partner gate** (worth ~$12-25K/yr once crossed), with TikTok >60s edits + a YouTube Shorts/VOD channel as slow-building secondary revenue ($10-100/mo at first), Kicks/gifted-sub culture as the immediate on-platform monetization, and no gambling kickback deals.
