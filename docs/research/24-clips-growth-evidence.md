# Research: clips-growth-evidence

_Business-model sweep, research date 2026-08-15/16. Nearly every dollar figure in this space is self-reported by streamers who profit from inflating it — treat headline numbers as marketing, verified numbers are flagged as such._

## Summary

The clips-to-streaming funnel is real but brutally lopsided: every documented breakout (Jynxzi, CaseOh, Sketch, IShowSpeed, PirateSoftware, TheBurntPeanut) came from high-VOLUME short-form posting (usually self-run, own footage, 1+ post/day) sustained for months before a nonlinear inflection, and the best-documented case (PirateSoftware) went from 379 to 8,500+ average Twitch viewers in ~4 months off daily YouTube Shorts. However, conversion is thin — even successful channels convert only ~0.005%–2.4% of followers into concurrent live viewers (Streams Charts), viral spikes decay within ~72 hours unless the stream content matches the clip promise, and most streamers with big clip views see little live growth. For a small Kick streamer, the evidence supports 1–3 self-posted clips/day with hard channel-name branding, schedule alignment, and a 3–6 month minimum horizon before judging results.

## Key facts

- PirateSoftware (best-documented conversion case): started posting one YouTube Short daily in Nov 2023 at ~19,000 subs; hit 343,000 subs by Nov 11 and ~814,000 by early Dec (monthly views went ~30,000 → ~200 million). Twitch average viewers: 379 (Oct 2023) → 2,808 (Nov 2023) → 8,500+ (Feb 2024), a reported +1,036% in 3 months. Self-run, own footage, no paid promotion. (Verified via mein-mmo reporting on his own statements + third-party stats.)
- CaseOh: posted his first TikTok Sept 10, 2022 and was live on Twitch two days later; Twitch Partner by Nov 2022 (~2 months); 8th fastest-growing Twitch streamer Q3 2023 with ~13K avg viewers; individual self-posted TikToks hit 18M plays (T-Rex runner clip, Sept 2023) and 5M plays (Granny clip); now ~5.8M Twitch followers and ~50K CCV. Self-posted clips were the engine from week one. (Verified via Know Your Meme timeline + StreamsCharts.)
- Jynxzi: streamed from Jan 2019 with ~365 streams at 1 viewer before TikTok clips took off in 2022-2023; reached 1M Twitch followers AND #1 most-subscribed on Twitch (80,000 active subs) on Apr 12, 2023; 10.2M followers by May 2026. His 2026 data shows content mix drives follower conversion: 27 hours of Valorant produced ~151K followers — 5.5x faster follower growth per hour than his home game Siege. (Verified via Wikipedia/Forbes/StreamsCharts.)
- Follower-to-live-viewer conversion is tiny even when the funnel works: Streams Charts (Jul-Aug 2023) found average concurrent viewers as % of Twitch followers ranged from 2.37% (best, RebeuDeter) to 0.004% (worst); chatters-to-followers is ~4.5% at 100K-500K followers and falls to ~1.75% at 10M+. Engagement trends DOWN once a channel passes ~100K followers. (Verified, published analysis.)
- Small-streamer viral conversions are real but modest in absolute terms: Chetter72 went from ~100 followers/single-digit viewers to 265,000+ followers in under a week (June 2024, ~20K/day) after a fan posted his Disneyland backstory clip to TikTok — but live viewership settled at hundreds average / ~3,000 peak, i.e. roughly 0.1-1% of the new followers actually watch live. Frankiep423: ~5 avg viewers for 8 months → one viral TikTok → 100 then 1,000+ peak viewers, ~40+ avg after. NateBosa: 9 viewers → 1.1K after one viral TikTok plus Jake Lucky amplification. (Verified via StreamsCharts/Dexerto/Unilad/Sportskeeda.)
- Fan-run clips built IShowSpeed and Kai Cenat: Speed streamed from Dec 2017 to ~10 viewers as late as 2020, then fan-posted TikTok memes of his rage reactions took him from 100K YouTube subs (Apr 2021) to 1M (June 2021). Kai Cenat imported a pre-built YouTube/AMP audience to Twitch in Feb 2021; clips spread via fan channels amplified an already-large base (283,245 peak CCV by Nov 2022). Neither is a from-zero clips case. (Verified.)
- Paid clipping is now an industrialized channel: Whop 'Content Rewards' campaigns pay $0.20-$6 per 1,000 verified views (marketplace average ~$1 CPM); MrBeast-backed Vyro (launched Oct 2025) pays flat $3/1,000 with $1,000/clip cap; Adin Ross promoted a Kick-exclusive clipper bounty on July 24, 2025 paying $50 per 100K views and $500 per 1M views. This buys VIEWS, not conversion — no published evidence it converts to live viewers at small scale. (Rates verified across multiple clipping-platform sources; conversion unproven.)
- Platforms themselves confirmed the off-platform discovery thesis: Twitch shipped a vertical clip editor with TikTok/YouTube export (May 2023) and rolled its TikTok-style Discovery Feed out to all users (April 2024) explicitly because discovery happens via short-form; Kick's Trainwreck promised a 'TikTok-like' trending clips system for discoverability. Kick's own native discovery is weak — guides uniformly say Kick growth must come from off-platform short-form. (Verified.)
- Timeline reality across all cases: inflection is nonlinear and late — Jynxzi ~3-4 years total (2 years at 1 viewer), Sketch ~9-12 months of TikTok posting before the Feb 2024 'What's up brother' explosion (then +400K Twitch followers in Q2 2024 alone), TheBurntPeanut failed for ~a year (TikTok banned 'dozens of times'), quit, rebranded as a 3D peanut avatar in 2024, and was #2 on Twitch by 2026. CaseOh (2 days to first traction) and PirateSoftware (~2 weeks) are outliers who hit an unexploited algorithm window. (Verified timelines.)
- Virality without infrastructure decays fast: viral traffic windows run ~72 hours; a 22-second fully-watched clip outperforms a 60-second clip watched to 30%; TikTok's own guidance puts the completion-rate sweet spot at 21-34 seconds (gaming skews 15-30s); and engagement quality beats raw views — one analysis: 200K views with 500 engaged comments outconverts 2.6M views with 200 passive comments. (Mixed: platform guidance verified, comment-conversion claim is single-source creator analysis.)

## Gotchas / reality check

- Severe survivorship bias: every named case is a lottery winner. There is no published base-rate study; the structural data (72.8% of Twitch streamers earn $0; <1-2% follower-to-live conversion even for winners) implies millions-of-views-with-no-growth is the DEFAULT outcome, not the exception.
- The two fastest cases exploited algorithm windows that are now closed or crowded: CaseOh hit TikTok gaming in Sept 2022 and PirateSoftware hit the YouTube-Shorts-push in Nov 2023. Expect slower curves in 2026; their mechanics (daily self-posting, content-stream match) transfer, their timelines do not.
- Several widely-quoted numbers are estimates or self-reported: Jynxzi's $120K-$191K/month income (third-party estimate), CaseOh's 50K CCV (fan site), the '15% viewer growth in 3 months' stat (marketing site, unverifiable), and all Whop/clipper earnings figures come from the clipping platforms themselves, which sell the dream.
- Virality can be manufactured or fake: Sketch's explosion required Jynxzi's audience (collab, not solo clips); N3on faced credible viewbotting accusations (18K→67K jump); paid clipping campaigns inflate view counts without producing a single live viewer. Big clip numbers on a channel are not evidence the funnel converted.
- Drama/rage-bait clips have the best distribution economics and the worst everything else: volatile hate-watch audiences, platform bans (TheBurntPeanut lost dozens of TikTok accounts), advertiser risk, and the drama travels without the channel name attached. For a small channel it is the highest-variance, lowest-moat strategy.
- Retention after viral spikes is essentially undocumented — no source tracks Chetter72/Frankiep423/NateBosa-style channels 6 months later. The 72-hour decay window is the only well-attested dynamic; assume most spike viewers churn unless the live product independently retains them.
- TikTok actively fights the off-platform funnel: no tappable outbound links for most accounts, algorithmic preference for TikTok LIVE over external streaming, and platform-native viewers who rarely leave the app. Kick links are strictly worse than 'search my name on Kick' burned into the video.
- Sources like tiktok.com/discover pages, caseoh.org, captionrizz, brandid.app are SEO/fan aggregators — used here only where they corroborate primary reporting (Forbes, Streams Charts, Know Your Meme dated entries, mein-mmo interviews).

## Full details

## 1. Case studies with numbers and timelines

### Jynxzi (Nicholas Stewart) — the canonical TikTok-clips-to-#1 story
- **Starting size:** Began streaming Rainbow Six Siege on Twitch **January 2019**; famously did roughly **365 streams to ~1 viewer** (Forbes). Started a YouTube channel Sept 2020 posting rage compilations.
- **Clip strategy:** Self-posted short-form rage/skill/personality clips to TikTok + YouTube, later amplified by a large ecosystem of fan accounts (@jynxzd, @dailyjynxzi_, "Jynxzi Clips Daily" etc.). High-energy on-stream persona meant anyone clicking through from a TikTok saw the same energy live — **clip content matched stream content**, which multiple analysts credit for his conversion.
- **Inflection:** Traction began 2022; on **April 12, 2023** he hit 1M Twitch followers and became **the most-subscribed streamer on Twitch (80,000 active subs)**, passing Kai Cenat and xQc. Top streamer by hours watched Feb 2024. **10.2M Twitch followers / 6.5M YouTube subs by May 2026**. Estimated 2023 earnings $120K–$191K/month (estimate, unverified).
- **Time from start to inflection: ~3 years; from clip traction to #1: ~12–18 months.**
- 2026 data point on content mix: **188 hours of Siege → ~190K followers vs 27 hours of Valorant → ~151K followers (5.5x follower velocity per hour)** — novelty/variety converts follows faster than the "home" game (Streams Charts).

### CaseOh — fastest documented clip→stream loop
- **Sept 10, 2022:** first TikTok (NBA 2K). **Sept 11–12, 2022:** first Twitch stream, i.e. he stood up the funnel in ~48 hours. **Twitch Partner by Nov 2022** (~2 months).
- **Clip strategy:** entirely **self-posted** to his own @caseohgames TikTok; every stream produced next-day clip inventory. Viral posts: Granny horror clip **5M+ plays** (Aug 2023), T-Rex Runner clip **18M plays / 2.3M likes in two months** (Sept 2023). 2.3M TikTok followers by Oct 2023.
- **Q3 2023:** 8th fastest-growing streamer on Twitch, ~**13K average viewers**. Today ~5.8M Twitch followers, ~9.1M TikTok, **~50K average concurrents** (fan-site figures, directionally consistent with StreamsCharts). Won Best Variety Streamer + Gamer of the Year at the 2024 Streamer Awards ~24 months after his first stream.

### Sketch (Kylie Cox) — fan/collab-driven virality on top of grind
- Early–mid **2023**: streaming Madden NFL 24 **on TikTok** itself; July 2023 dual-streaming TikTok+Twitch; YouTube Nov 2023. So: ~9–12 months of posting/streaming before inflection.
- **Dec 22, 2023:** fan impersonation TikTok hits 5.1M plays — first sign of meme energy. **Feb 29, 2024:** Jynxzi FaceTimes him on stream; the "What's up brother?" clip explodes (one repost: **7.5M plays in 17 days**, March 2024).
- **Q2 2024: +400,000 Twitch followers in the quarter → 1.3M total** (Streams Charts fastest-growing list); mainstream crossover (NFL teams, the Masters using the catchphrase). Key mechanism: **a bigger streamer's clip machine (Jynxzi's) pointed at him** — collab-borne virality, not solo clip posting.

### IShowSpeed — pure fan-clip flywheel
- Streaming since **Dec 2017**; **~10 concurrent viewers in 2020** (3+ years of nothing). In 2021 **fan-posted TikTok memes** of his rage reactions detonated: **100K YouTube subs (Apr 2021) → 1M (June 2021)**. He did not run the clip machine himself; the content was so meme-able fans did it for free. Now 60M+ cross-platform followers.

### Kai Cenat — imported audience + fan clip amplification
- YouTube from Jan 2018; joined **AMP mid-2020**; moved to Twitch **Feb 2021** with an existing audience. Clips on TikTok/Shorts created a "feedback loop where each viral moment pushed more people to tune in live" (Streams Charts), plus celebrity collabs (Bobby Shmurda Apr 2022, Lil Baby Oct 2022, 21 Savage Nov 2022 → **283,245 peak CCV**). Not a from-zero clips case — clips amplified momentum he already had.

### PirateSoftware (Jason "Thor" Hall) — the cleanest replicable playbook
- **Nov 2023:** commits to **one YouTube Short every day, posted at noon Pacific** ("lunchtime for West Coast tech workers"), cut from his own Twitch dev/GameDev Q&A footage.
- YouTube: **~19K subs Nov 1 → 343K Nov 11 → ~814K by early Dec 2023** (+~800K in one month); monthly views **~30K → ~200M**. Later crossed 2.5M subs.
- **Twitch conversion: 379 avg viewers (Oct 2023) → 2,808 (Nov 2023) → 8,500+ (Feb 2024) — +1,036% in 3 months** (mein-mmo citing stream analytics). This is the single best publicly documented shorts-views→live-viewers conversion series. Notes: talking-head advice clips (personality/expertise), not gameplay; zero paid spend; his 2025 reputation collapse is unrelated to the growth mechanics.

### TheBurntPeanut — 2024–2026 proof the loop still works
- Tried TikTok as a normal facecam Tarkov streamer; **account banned dozens of times; nothing clicked; quit for ~a year**. 2024: rebranded behind a motion-captured **3D peanut avatar**; clips of the absurd avatar + FPS skill spread on TikTok/Shorts. Twitch following **tripled in H2 2025** (Tarkov + ARC Raiders), **60.2M hours watched in 2025** (top VTuber), **#2 on all of Twitch early 2026** ahead of Jynxzi in hours watched at times; 2M+ Twitch followers, 1.1M TikTok followers by Mar 2026. Lesson: the differentiated visual hook fixed a funnel that years of generic content couldn't.

### Small-scale one-clip cases (closest analog to a 5-viewer channel)
- **Chetter72 (June 2024):** ~100 followers, single-digit viewers. A viewer (KindPenguin7) asked his backstory (former Disneyland "Crush the Turtle" voice actor, lost the job to medical issues), tipped $450, and posted the clip to TikTok. Result: **+265,000 followers in under a week (~20K/day)**, top follower-gainer on Twitch June 14–20, 2024. Live viewership after: **hundreds average, ~3,000 peak** — i.e., ~0.1–1% of viral followers became live viewers.
- **Frankiep423 (2023):** ~**5 average viewers for 8 months** → one viral TikTok → first 100-viewer stream, then **1,000+ peak**; average settled around 40+ initially. Wholesome moment (brought his mom on to celebrate).
- **NateBosa:** 9 viewers → **1.1K viewers** overnight after a TikTok went viral and the community/Jake Lucky amplified it.

### N3on (Kick) — the drama path
- Built ~**31K average Kick viewers** (30-day StreamsCharts window) on loud, chaotic, deliberately provocative content; clips spread across TikTok/X/Shorts/Reels largely via repost accounts, including people posting to mock him ("criticism itself creates visibility" — hate-watch analysis). Costs: **Nov 2023 viewbotting accusations (18K→67K viewer jump)**, repeated bans/controversies (e.g., "Notti Bop," Aug 2023), advertiser risk, and an audience that watches to jeer.

## 2. Conversion data (views → follows → live viewers)

Hard published numbers are scarce; here's everything found, ranked by reliability:

- **Streams Charts follower-engagement study (Jul–Aug 2023), verified:** average concurrent viewers as a % of Twitch followers peaks around 3.4–4M followers and spans **2.37% (best) to 0.004% (worst)**; chatter-to-follower ratio **4.5%** at 100K–500K followers, **~3%** at 1M–5M, **1.75%** at 10M+. Engagement declines steadily past 100K followers. Practical read: **a follow acquired from a clip is worth ~1/50th to 1/1000th of a live viewer.**
- **PirateSoftware series (verified via reporting):** ~200M shorts views/month coincided with +2,400 avg concurrents within the first month, ~+8,100 by month 4. Back-of-envelope: **roughly 1 sustained live viewer per ~25,000–80,000 monthly short-form views** during a hyper-viral run.
- **Chetter72 (verified):** 265K viral follows → hundreds of average live viewers = **<1% follower→live conversion**, consistent with the Streams Charts curve.
- **YouTube "3x discovery rule" (Streams Charts 2026 platform guide, plausible but methodology unpublished):** Shorts viewers are ~3x more likely to be shown your live stream within the next hour — the argument for posting Shorts right before/during going live on YouTube-linked funnels.
- **"Streamers actively sharing clips on TikTok see ~15% viewer growth within three months on average"** — circulating marketing claim (buycheapestfollowers.com); **unverified, low-quality source, treat as rumor**.
- **Paid-view pricing (verified across clipping platforms):** Whop Content Rewards $0.20–$6 per 1,000 verified views (avg ~$1 CPM; e.g., Roobet $1.50/1K with $250K budget); Vyro (MrBeast-backed, Oct 2025) flat $3/1K, $1,000/clip cap; Adin Ross's Kick-exclusive bounty (announced live to 60K viewers, July 24, 2025): **$50 per 100K views, $500 per 1M views**. TikTok Creator Rewards pays creators roughly "a few hundred dollars per million qualified views." None of these publish live-viewer conversion — they price *reach*, not *retention*.
- **Twitch's own behavior as evidence:** vertical clip editor with TikTok/Shorts export (May 2023), Discovery Feed rollout to all users (Apr 2024), Instagram clip export — the platform re-architected around the fact that discovery happens off-platform in short-form.

## 3. What clip content converts (vs. just gets views)

- **Personality with a repeatable hook converts best.** Sketch's "What's up brother?", CaseOh's food-adjacent rage humor, TheBurntPeanut's peanut avatar, Speed's bark — all are *bits a new viewer will see again within minutes of joining the live stream*. The consistent finding across Jynxzi/CaseOh analyses: conversion happens when the stream instantly delivers what the clip promised.
- **Skill alone is weak; skill+reaction is strong.** Jynxzi is a genuinely high-level Siege player, but the clips that traveled were rage/reactions wrapped around the gameplay. His 2026 numbers (Valorant novelty converting followers 5.5x faster per hour than Siege) show the *moment*, not the mastery, drives follows.
- **Wholesome/authentic story clips produce the biggest single-spike conversions at small scale** — Chetter72 ($450 donation + backstory), Frankiep423 (mom celebration), NateBosa, and the "they finally put faith in kid streamer" wave were all sympathy/authenticity clips that made viewers *want the streamer to win*, which converts to follows AND short-term live tune-in. Retention past the 72-hour wave is undocumented in every case.
- **Drama/rage-bait gets the most raw distribution** (reposters, haters, and mockers all distribute for free — the N3on hate-watch dynamic) and it does monetize, but: audiences are volatile and adversarial, brand risk is high, platforms ban accounts (TheBurntPeanut lost dozens of TikTok accounts in his earlier era), and view counts get polluted by bot accusations. For a small channel, drama also has no moat — the clip goes viral, the *drama* is remembered, not the channel.
- **Engagement quality beats view count:** one creator-economy analysis found 200K views with 500 engaged comments outconverts 2.6M views with 200 passive comments ("views vs comments" — single source, plausible). Comments = people who processed *who you are*, not just the moment.
- **Volume of modest clips beats one fluke:** small streamers grew most through compounding (cited example: 29 posts at a few hundred–few thousand views each outperforming a single 100K-view fluke) because repeated exposure on FYP/Shorts is what makes someone hit follow (Eklipse small-streamer analysis).

## 4. Failure base rate — "TikTok famous, 5 viewers"

- No formal study quantifies the failure rate, but the structural numbers imply it's the default outcome: **72.8% of Twitch streamers reportedly earn $0** and ~1% ever reach Partner (secondary source, treat as approximate); meanwhile the follower→viewer curve above means even a *successful* viral wave converts <1–2% to live viewership.
- **The 72-hour decay:** viral traffic windows run ~72 hours; if the streamer isn't live repeatedly inside that window with content matching the clip, the wave passes. Chetter72 converted well *because he was live daily while the wave crested*.
- **Documented failure modes:**
  1. **Content mismatch** — clip is a meme, stream is a quiet grind. Analyses of Jynxzi explicitly credit his conversion to the fact that TikTok viewers "were almost guaranteed to see the loud entertaining personality from the TikToks as soon as they joined."
  2. **No identity attached** — fan reposts and faceless clip channels harvest the views; r/LivestreamFail-sourced clip channels (ninocentx, Nostradamus) built whole YouTube businesses on other people's moments. Without a burned-in name, the *moment* goes viral, not the channel.
  3. **Platform-native audiences** — "1M views, under 10K follows" is a documented TikTok phenomenon; most TikTok viewers won't leave the app, and TikTok algorithmically suppresses outbound links (link-in-bio only, no tappable links in comments for most accounts).
  4. **Wrong platform economics** — TikTok pushes its own LIVE product; a TikTok-viral streamer gets funneled toward TikTok LIVE, not Kick. Kick specifically has weak native discovery (all guides agree Kick growth must be manufactured off-platform), so a broken off-platform funnel means zero growth.
  5. **Big TikTok ≠ big anything else:** engagement ratios fall as follower counts rise (Streams Charts), and even mega-streamers find their own TikToks underperforming their fame (Lirik publicly showing shocked-level low TikTok stats vs YouTube).

## 5. Mechanics that improve conversion (evidence-graded)

- **Burned-in channel name/watermark on every clip + same handle everywhere** — universal recommendation (StreamChat AI Kick guide: "Kick username visible in every clip and every social bio"); the counterfactual is documented (clip channels capturing value from unbranded moments). Cost: zero. For Kick, watermark "kick.com/WinslowBankz" since TikTok won't give you a tappable link.
- **Schedule alignment / predictability** — 3–4 fixed weekly slots recommended over sporadic daily; conversion requires the clicker to find you live either *now* or at a stated time. Put the schedule in bio + pinned comment.
- **Post while live / just before going live** — strongest platform-mechanical support on YouTube (the "3x discovery rule": Shorts viewers ~3x more likely to be surfaced your live within the hour). On TikTok, accounts that go LIVE get the profile-ring indicator, and pinned comments during LIVE guide traffic. Twitch's own Discovery Feed surfaces clips → live. PirateSoftware's noon-PT daily slot shows timing-to-audience matters more than timing-to-algorithm.
- **Pinned comment + bio CTA** — TikTok suppresses external links, so successful funnels use "search my name on Kick," pinned comments, and profile bios; evidence is practitioner-consensus rather than measured.
- **Clip length: 21–34 seconds** (TikTok Creator Portal completion-rate guidance; gaming clips skew 15–30s); full completion of a short clip beats partial completion of a long one — completion rate is the ranked signal.
- **Volume + consistency:** every successful case posted at least daily during the growth phase (CaseOh, PirateSoftware 1/day minimum; Jynxzi's ecosystem posted many/day across accounts). One short a day was enough for PirateSoftware when the hook was strong.
- **Paid clippers (Whop/Vyro model):** buys reach at ~$1–3 per 1,000 views; economically rational only once you know your clips convert, because you pay for views, not viewers. Adin Ross's rates ($0.50/1K effective) show even mega-streamers price clip views cheaply.
- **Collab clipping:** the single fastest documented accelerant at every scale — Sketch via Jynxzi, Jynxzi via Tyler1 (Dantes' post-collab averages +217%, peaks +246% in the same Streams Charts dataset). At small scale: clip collabs with slightly bigger streamers and tag them.

## 6. Realistic timeline for a small streamer in 2026

- **Base case from community + guide consensus:** "it takes longer than anyone warns you about" (Kick growth guide); no credible source promises movement in under ~90 days. The one circulating quantified claim (~15% viewer growth in 3 months for active clip posters) is from a low-quality source — treat as an optimistic ceiling for the average case, not a floor.
- **Distribution of documented outcomes:** 2 days (CaseOh, 2022 algorithm window — no longer replicable), ~2 weeks (PirateSoftware, Nov 2023 Shorts push — window now crowded), ~9–12 months (Sketch, before collab detonated it), ~2–4 years (Jynxzi, IShowSpeed, TheBurntPeanut including a failed first act). Median realistic expectation for daily posting with a real hook: **first measurable live-viewer movement in 3–6 months; life-changing inflection, if it comes, is usually a single unpredictable clip or collab landing on top of 6–18 months of inventory.**
- **Posting volume from the successful cases:** minimum 1/day sustained without gaps (PirateSoftware's explicit rule); CaseOh-style 1–3/day from every stream; 5–15 clip candidates exist per stream (industry tooling estimate), so daily streaming supports daily posting indefinitely.
- **2026-specific notes for Kick:** Kick's native discovery is still weak — off-platform short-form is effectively the *only* small-channel growth channel; upside is first-mover room (few clip channels cover Kick streamers) and the 95/5 sub split makes each converted viewer worth ~2x a Twitch viewer. Kick's TikTok-like trending clips feed (promised by Trainwreck) partially shipped but is not a substitute for TikTok/Shorts/Reels distribution.
- **What to measure instead of views:** profile taps and follows per 1K views, comment density (engaged comments predict conversion better than views), and live CCV in the 72 hours after any clip >50K views. If a 1M-view clip produces <100 follows and no CCV bump, the funnel (branding/CTA/content match) is broken — the views are not the problem.
